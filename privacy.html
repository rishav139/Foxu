Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Foxu ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you visit the Foxu website and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Website analytics: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages they view. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally. Cloudflare may process your IP address to provide the service.
  • Feedback: If you choose to share feedback, we receive your responses through a Google Form. This may include any details you type into it.
  • Email: If you contact us at foxu.original@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

2. How we use it

  • To understand how the website is used and to improve it.
  • To read your feedback and respond to your questions.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Forms: Google Privacy Policy

4. How long we keep it

  • Analytics data: kept by Cloudflare under its own retention terms.
  • Feedback and emails: kept for 12 months, then deleted.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us at foxu.original@gmail.com, and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Children

Foxu is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows when it last changed.
