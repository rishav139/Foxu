The Collection

Seven flavours

Pink Guava Chilli

Sweet · Spicy

Delicately tossed in quality olive oil and coated with pink guava powder, a lively blend of chillies, and a balanced touch of pink and black salt. A delightful harmony of fruity sweetness and gentle heat.

Cheese & Garlic

Savoury · Cheesy

Lightly coated with quality olive oil and tossed with rich cheese seasoning, aromatic garlic, a hint of chilli, and balanced salts. A savoury, creamy flavour with a delicious garlic finish.

Curry Leaves & Chilli Garlic

Bold · Aromatic

Crisp foxnuts tossed in quality olive oil with fragrant curry leaf powder, aromatic garlic, warming chilli, and a touch of pink and black salt. A bold, savoury twist inspired by South Indian flavours.

Pani Puri

Tangy · Street-style

Coated with quality olive oil and tossed in a tangy blend of tamarind powder, cumin, chaat masala, and aromatic spices. Inspired by India’s favourite street-food flavours, with a zesty, chatpata finish.

Margarita

Zesty · Herby

Lightly tossed in quality olive oil and seasoned with tomato, fragrant basil, oregano, chilli flakes, and pizza-inspired spices. A deliciously savoury blend with familiar Italian-inspired notes.

Sour Cream & Onion

Creamy · Tangy

Gently coated with quality olive oil and tossed in a creamy sour cream and onion seasoning, complemented by delicate chive notes and balanced salts. Smooth, savoury, and satisfyingly classic.

Himalayan Salt

Mild · Classic

Simply tossed in quality olive oil and finished with Himalayan pink salt and a touch of black salt. An elegant, understated flavour that lets the natural crunch of premium foxnuts shine.
